About Us

Who we are, what the News AI Engine is, and why we write about AI video automation.

News AI Engine is an independent technology publication written by the team that designs, builds and operates the News AI Engine: a production system that researches, scripts, narrates, edits, checks and schedules news explainer videos for YouTube and TikTok.

We started this site because most writing about “AI video automation” is either marketing or theory. We had something different: a working engine, a long list of failures, and the logs, measurements and fixes that came out of them. This site turns that experience into guides that other creators and developers can actually use.

What the News AI Engine does

The engine is a set of Python services that run on modest hardware - including, for much of its life, an Android phone running Termux. In one pass it can:

  • generate a narration script from a headline with large language models, with factual guard-rails and a multi-provider fallback router;
  • turn the script into speech with a consistent synthetic voice;
  • align every spoken word to the audio with speech recognition, so captions and graphics appear exactly when words are said;
  • scan a footage library with vision models and match clips to what is being said;
  • cut a fast montage, design motion graphics, and render vertical (9:16) and widescreen (16:9) videos with FFmpeg;
  • run automated quality control, and schedule publishing through official platform APIs.

What we write about

  • AI news automation - pipelines, guard-rails, and where humans must stay in the loop.
  • Video editing - montage rhythm, captions, transitions and automated visual direction.
  • AI tools - honest evaluations of language, vision and speech models for creators.
  • Python and FFmpeg - the techniques that actually render our videos.
  • Remotion and motion graphics - programmatic design, and when lighter tools win.
  • Case studies - real problems we hit and how we fixed them.

Our principles

  1. Experience first. We write about what we have built, run and measured.
  2. Accuracy over hype. We say what failed, what it cost and what we are unsure about.
  3. Useful to readers. Every article should leave you able to do something you could not do before.
  4. Responsible AI. We use AI to assist our work, never to invent facts, and we explain exactly how in our Editorial Policy.

Privacy and independence

We never publish credentials, private production data or details of the channels the engine serves. We are not affiliated with Google, YouTube, TikTok, the Remotion project or any AI provider we mention. If the site shows advertising, it never influences what we write.
