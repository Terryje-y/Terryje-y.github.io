How it works

From headline to video in three steps

Write the story

AI drafts a short, factual script from your headline and notes. You stay in control of every claim.

Add voice and visuals

The script becomes a voiceover, then footage, motion graphics and captions are timed to every word.

Check and publish

Automatic quality checks run first. Then you post, schedule, or save the video to your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this blog for?

Creators, small newsrooms and curious beginners who want to make news and explainer videos with AI. Our guides start in plain English. Deeper technical articles are clearly labelled for readers who want them.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Start with the Start here page and the Creator Guides. Coding is only needed if you want to build your own tools, and our tutorials cover that step by step.

What is the News AI Engine?

It is the AI video production system our team builds and uses. It writes scripts, creates voiceovers, edits footage and adds captions. Read more on the Engine page.

Is AI-made news trustworthy?

Only with clear rules. We never let AI invent quotes, claims or evidence, and a human reviews every story. Our editorial policy explains how we work.

Is the content free?

Yes. Every article on this site is free to read.
