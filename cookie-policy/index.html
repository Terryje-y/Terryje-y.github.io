Advertising cookies

If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may set cookies (for example __gads, __gpi and IDE) to show ads, limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance and - where you have consented - personalise ads. See Google’s explanation at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Managing cookies

  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies.
  • Opt out of personalised Google ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of many third-party ad cookies at www.aboutads.info.

Blocking cookies does not stop you reading the Site.
