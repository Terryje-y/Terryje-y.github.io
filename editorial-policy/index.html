Editorial Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Our goal is simple: publish the most useful, honest explanation of how AI video automation really works.

Where our content comes from

Every article is based on first-hand work with the News AI Engine: code we wrote, systems we operate, errors we logged, and measurements we took on real hardware. When we describe a result - a render time, a failure rate, an API limit - it comes from our own runs or from the official documentation we cite.

How we use AI

We build AI tools, and we use them carefully in our own writing:

  • AI may help us outline an article, summarise our internal technical notes, or suggest clearer wording.
  • A person reviews, tests and edits every article before it is published. Code is run, commands are checked, and numbers are compared with our logs.
  • AI is never used to invent facts, quotations, statistics, sources or experiences.
  • We do not mass-produce articles. We publish when we have something worth saying.

Accuracy and testing

  • Tutorials are tested on the setup they describe; we state that setup clearly (for example, Python version, FFmpeg version and operating system).
  • When we evaluate a tool or model, we describe how we tested it and its limits.
  • We link to official documentation for platform rules and API details, because those can change.

Privacy and security

We never publish credentials, private production data, personal data, or details of the channels our engine serves. Code samples are simplified and stripped of secrets before publication, and an automated check blocks publication if anything that looks like a credential or private detail slips through.

Independence

We are not paid to cover products. If we ever receive a product or have a commercial relationship relevant to an article, we will disclose it in that article. Advertising never influences coverage.

Corrections and updates

If we get something wrong, we fix it and add an “Updated” date. Material corrections are noted in the article. Tell us about errors through our Contact page.
