Privacy Policy

How News AI Engine collects, uses and protects information, including cookies and advertising by Google.

Last updated November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how News AI Engine (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit https://terryje-y.github.io (the “Site”).

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you contact us by email or through a contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. We use it only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, date and time) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify individuals.

Cookies and local storage. We store a small preference in your browser to remember that you dismissed our cookie notice. Advertising partners may also use cookies, described below.

AI Video Studio (free browser tool)

The AI Video Studio runs entirely in your web browser.

  • Your API key is used only to send your requests directly from your browser to Google’s Gemini API. If you tick “Remember on this device”, it is stored in your browser’s local storage on that device only. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the key field and pressing Save, or by clearing your browser data.
  • Your headlines, notes and scripts are sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate text and audio, under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store them.
  • Your photos and video clips never leave your device. The video is assembled in your browser.
  • Use of the Gemini API with your key is subject to Google’s terms, and any usage limits or charges on your Google account are between you and Google.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. In connection with this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info and, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using a consent message provided through Google’s certified consent management tools.

How we use information

  • to operate, secure and improve the Site;
  • to respond to your messages;
  • to show advertising that helps keep the Site free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and then deleted within 24 months. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider according to its own retention policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at the address on our Contact page with “Privacy request” in the subject line and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The Site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Third-party links

Articles link to external websites and documentation. Their privacy practices are their own; please review their policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the address on our Contact page, or see our Contact page.
